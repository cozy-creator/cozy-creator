`cozy machine end` says a rental shut down only once its Hub affirms it ended. Signed out, holding a revoked key, or refused by the Hub, it exits non-zero naming why, and `cozy machine ls` and the page show the rental as not shut down and possibly billing until it is ended again (it used to print "shut down" while the pod kept billing). `cozy logout` revokes only a key this computer enrolled; a key installed with `cozy login --key` is forgotten here and left valid for the other computers that hold it. A pod signs in with its own key: `cozy login --export pod.key` here, `cozy login --key pod.key` there (cl-875).
