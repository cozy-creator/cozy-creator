A CUDA graph captured under varena (`tensorfs.varena.torch.graph`) no longer faults the GPU when a tensor it reads was freed (var-034). Its replay refuses in one line: "a tensor this CUDA graph reads was freed … hold its inputs as long as the graph replays, or record it again". Before, varena could unmap that memory under demand, and the replay read unmapped addresses: `gpu_fault … Xid 31`, three times in a bench whose operands were held only by a dropped `partial` (pod-session 44416217064). A raw `graph.replay()` is checked too. Weights are never held for a graph: a Region's pages are re-pinned for each replay. A kernel launched by pointer outside Torch is not seen: its caller holds what it launches on.
