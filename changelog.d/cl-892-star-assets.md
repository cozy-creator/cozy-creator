Star an asset: Star / Unstar in a box's right-click menu sets `starred: true` on it, kept in `project.db` (Calcifer the only writer) and in its YAML, with Undo. A star is no input, so it puts nothing out of date, and it is not an approval. The project's Files drawer (main menu, Files) lists every asset with its picture, name and kind, starred first with a ★; a click opens one on the canvas, and a right click downloads it.
