An encrypted project's lock, decrypt, encrypt, rotate, rename and move to the trash wait their turn on the project rather than refuse "try again in a moment" while a page reads it or a render's runs read their journal, and a lock no longer waits on a request in flight: once it has the project, new reads of it are refused as locked, and a file still streaming from its view is cut. A lock at an idle or maximum deadline that something still holds (a program with a file open in the view) is forced closed after 60 s, its view unmounted and its key let go, and the log says once what held it.
