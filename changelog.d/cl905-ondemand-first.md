A running job's on-demand weights no longer lose the link to a later job's downloads. An executor reads a model it declares on demand (Qwen Image's prompt expander) through its own fetch, outside the machine's download line. Before, a queued job's download, a project's fetch ahead or a warm download kept the link: on one rental, Qwen's prompt upsampling waited 423 s for its expander while H3's fetch ahead downloaded 30 GB, and the expander's own decode took 8 s. Now while another process sharing the machine's store is fetching, TensorD starts no object for a queued run, a fetch ahead, a guess or a warm set, and pauses those in flight at their next bytes; they go on from where they stopped once the read ends.
