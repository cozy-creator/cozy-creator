A lock, or a project's folder gone, never waits on a reader outside Cozy any more. TensorD's machine journal and Calcifer's state.db empty their WAL with one rule (`platform::durable::wal`): each holds off its own readers, checkpoints once without waiting, and when a reader outside the process still holds an older snapshot, says the emptying is owed (`…: its WAL still holds deleted rows' pages, …; it's emptied after the next write`) and finishes it after its next write or at its next start. Before, each retried for up to 40 × 5 s, then TensorD gave up without a word and Calcifer failed the lock (`vault_index`) or left an owed clean-up's view behind (xs-220, hack audit tensord #3 and calcifer-cli #7).
When a lock leaves state.db's emptying owed, `cozy vault lock` says so once (`another program (sqlitebrowser, pid 4242) has this computer's project index open, so the project's id stays in the index's write log until that program closes`), naming the program on Linux from `/proc/locks` (no file of the index is opened: closing one would drop SQLite's own locks). An owed emptying is retried by the next write, and later at most once a second.
