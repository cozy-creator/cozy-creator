varena packs small weight regions into shared pages (cr-317). A region its set names (`Device.region(bytes, source, set)`), whose rounding to the card's 2 MiB pages would waste over 1/16 of it, sits back to back with the set's others in one address range. Its pages are created on its fill, shared with its neighbours, and released when no resident region is left on them; reclaim counts the bytes actually released. Qwen3.6-35B-A3B's 10,496 int8 experts took 4 MiB each for 3.01 MiB of weights (45.2 GiB for 35.0 GiB); packed they take their own size, with no joined units.
