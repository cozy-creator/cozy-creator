`cozy open` opens the project in your default browser (`$BROWSER`, else `xdg-open`, `open` or `start`) and prints its address; with no browser to start it prints the address and says so in one line. Cozy no longer starts its own private Chrome, so a Calcifer restart or update leaves your tabs open: they reconnect, and an encrypted project's shows its locked page. Before a lock, a storage change or a stop closes an encrypted project, its open tabs send what was just typed first (cl-1067).
