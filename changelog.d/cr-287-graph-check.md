A CUDA graph's replay that differs from its call says how big the difference is: `replay_mismatch` names the first differing value, the largest |difference| and the relative L2 beside the count (TensorFS `replay_differs`). A dev machine (`dev = true` at the top of its config.toml) tells its executors so in their Start, and the Runtime audits every 16th replay of a region against its call as written. The release gate's `graphs` suite holds CUDA graphs to their calls bit for bit on such a pod (cr-287, cr-300, cr-283).
