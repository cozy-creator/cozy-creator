Where nothing of a tool ran on a GPU and its release has no benchmark of that GPU, its time is estimated from its benchmarks of other GPUs, each scaled by how the two GPUs compare across the tools both were measured on (a 4090 typically takes about 1.4 times a 5090's time), else by their spec sheets (th-377, Paul 11-15). Calcifer's API says such a time is `scaled`, `cozy run` says "estimated" in its placement line and a run's time left, and placement never counts it as measured.
