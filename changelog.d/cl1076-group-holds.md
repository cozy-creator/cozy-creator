TensorD: an idle multi-GPU executor's models can be evicted. Each rank opens what it loads under its own process, and TensorD counted only the group's first, so a group left idle kept its model through any render's need for room. Now every process of an idle executor counts as its holder, ending it ends the whole group, and room is judged once they have all exited. `cozy model install` makes room for everything it lacks before it downloads, as a render does: an install that fits by dropping bakes, unused models or idle executors' models lands, and one that can't fit is refused with the sizes before a byte lands. Before, it asked one object at a time and could be refused while an idle executor's models stood (cl-1076).
