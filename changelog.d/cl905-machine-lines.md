A machine now works through three lines at once, each in job order: installs, downloads and runs. The next job builds its environment and downloads its weights while this one runs. Downloads go one job at a time at full speed. A job's last bytes no longer share the link with the next job's first, and a queued job's downloads wait until the running or next job's are done. `cozy model install` and model adapters now download in their run's place in line, not ahead of every queued job. Environments build one at a time in job order, and a waiting run says so: `next in line to install, behind run …'s birefnet 0.3.0`. A function that loads no model itself (H3's `generate`) starts downloading what its internal functions read as soon as it is accepted, while it imports. Its weights after that are guesses: one is fetched only if it fits on the disk beside what comes first. A guess stops when the function's first call says which function it is, or when any run needs the room. A project's fetch ahead goes behind every run's own downloads and fetches the same way.
