A run that waits says what for, not the stage it waits in ("Preparing, time left unknown", read as stuck) (cl-1197): its box says what it waits for in words its line holds whole (`Waiting for 2 GPUs`, `Waiting for the CPU`, `Waiting for install`), and its machine's tab and queue say all of it, as its drawer and the run list do: `Waiting for 2 GPUs, held by run PRO6-60, about 2 min`. A run waiting on a download reads one way: its box `Waiting for download`, and its rows, its drawer and the run list `Waiting for Sdxl to download`, no longer beside its machine's own reason with its bytes. A box's line too long for its width ends on a whole word, never mid-word.
