TensorD's `appended_from` is the length of the leading parts a revision shares with the one before, digest and length alike, part by part from the start (cl-1210): a growing video keeps all of its last revision but its index, so Calcifer copies those bytes and reads only the new ones. A follower holding more than a revision keeps is reset; Calcifer says when kept bytes fail the whole file's digest and reads it whole.
