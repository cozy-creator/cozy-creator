A Qwen3.5-family VL model (`Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration`, `Qwen3_5MoeForConditionalGeneration`) imports as three components: `model`, `vision` (the vision tower, keys without `model.visual.`) and `mtp` (the multi-token-prediction head, keys as they are), so a text-only call downloads no vision tower. The split is data (`crates/tensorfs/converters/splits.json`) and composes with the Qwen3.6-35B-A3B experts table; `tfs export --safetensors` writes the upstream folder again. A re-import makes a new commit and its lanes are derived from it again; commits made before keep one component.
