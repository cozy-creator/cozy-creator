A retained state (a prefix's K/V) whose region finds no room on the card when it is written goes straight to its pinned host copy, from the tensors being retained, and its first use fills it from there as it fills an evicted one, instead of failing `memory_exhausted`. `varena.Retained.write` returns None then, and `Retained.ptr` is its device address either way. Qwen-Image 2's prefix K/V at a 6 GiB cap, 677 MB a layer beside the 4 GiB its step held (var-030).
