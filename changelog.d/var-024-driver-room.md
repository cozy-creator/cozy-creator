TensorFS leaves the driver only what it is still expected to allocate on its own for a process (kernel code at a first launch, local memory, libraries' handles): the most a process on the card held outside TensorFS's books, learned on the machine or taken from the release's table per card and driver, less what the process already holds there; with neither, its context's measured cost. A fixed 800 MiB was left before, 10-13% of an 8 GB card, kept free by evicting weights. Each run's memory record says what its process held outside the books (`out_of_books_bytes`), and `release-gate/ledger.py out-of-books` makes the release's table from them (var-024).
