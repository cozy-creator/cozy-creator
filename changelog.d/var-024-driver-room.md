TensorFS leaves the driver only what it is still expected to allocate on its own for a process (kernel code at a first launch, local memory, libraries' handles): the most a process running the same models on that card held outside TensorFS's books, learned on the machine and kept across reboots, less what the process already holds there; for models the card never ran, the process's measured context. What the driver takes during a run comes out of that room as it happens. A fixed 800 MiB was left before, 10-13% of an 8 GB card, kept free by evicting weights. A process coming up on a nearly full card asks the others there for that room. Each run's memory record says what its process held outside the books (`out_of_books_bytes`) (var-024).
