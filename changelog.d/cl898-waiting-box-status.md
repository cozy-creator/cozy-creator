A box that is rendering or waiting says how on its whole face: what it's doing in words, one bar for the whole render with its time left, and Cancel; then its machine and GPU (or the card being rented and its boot), what it's doing with its numbers (its place in line and what's ahead, inputs uploading, its package installing, weights arriving at their rate, each stage's steps, a decode's tokens, its result downloading), and what it warned of. A failure says why on the box. Cancel stops it in any phase, says Cancelling… until its run ends, and Undo renders it again.

A decode's progress (`unit: "tokens"`, from the Runtime) is a count, never steps: `Upsampling prompt · 65 tokens`, with no fraction or time left from its cap, in the page, `cozy run` and Calcifer's estimate. A stage reads by one readable-name map (`Denoising · step 6 of 8`, `Segment 2 of 3 · Decoding video · frame 173 of 350`, `Rewriting the prompt · 65 tokens`): a count is called a step only where it denoises, and an unknown one is just a count. Open, a box's progress sits between its name and its picture, large, its bar and time left the whole render's (its parts too) and the part it's in under it. A run's log warnings are on its record while it goes, and a run that rents says what it rents until its machine is named. A take whose file is still coming says `Downloading the result` with its bytes, rate and time left, and isn't shown approved until the file is here.

Every error the page shows (a note, a box's failure, a run's drawer) is text to select and has a Copy that puts it whole on the clipboard: what it said, its code, its run and machine, `cozy run watch <n>`, what to do and its traceback. A note stays up while it's hovered or its text is selected.
