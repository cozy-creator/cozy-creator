A Video Timeline's frame rate, aspect ratio and resolution are its fields, under its box and in its timeline's bar in Focus, no longer in its right-click menu. A new frame rate keeps each clip's time whoever sets it: `cozy apply` now moves an Edit's in and out points, and a still's length, as the page does, and `cozy diff` says each that moves. `cozy apply` takes those three as the page offers them: 24, 25, 30, 48, 50 or 60 fps, a standard ratio, 720p to 2160p.
