A Video Timeline's frame rate, aspect ratio and resolution are its fields, under its box and in its timeline's bar in Focus, no longer in its right-click menu. A new frame rate keeps each clip's time whoever sets it: `cozy apply` now moves an Edit's in and out points, and a still's length, as the page does, and `cozy diff` says each that moves. Their menus offer presets, NTSC's 23.976, 29.97 and 59.94 fps among them; `cozy apply`, the page and the API take any rate, ratio or size the cut can use, saying so in a line when it isn't a preset, and refuse only what it can't (fps 0, say), in one line.
