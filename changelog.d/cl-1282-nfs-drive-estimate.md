A machine with a datacenter NFS drive (RunPod) is quoted for what the drive holds as a read off the drive, at the rate its downloads
measured reading it, rather than as a download from the Hub at its link (cl-1282). Its Status says the drive's rate and, per checkpoint,
what the drive holds of what its store lacks, including a checkpoint a function read there before a restart. Prefetch waits on the same
times. A machine that has never seen a model (a fresh pod, or a rental not yet up) is still quoted the whole download.
