Every tool's models and LoRAs are set in one place, Models & LoRAs, the picker image tools use: at the bottom of a box's fields, or beside its tool when none show, with what's set listed under it; no fine-tune or LoRA rows elsewhere. A child call's slots of one parameter are one choice (H3's Base Model sets every segment). The tool is named once under the media, "<Package> · <Tool>". In Focus a tool's fields all show, with no More or Advanced.
