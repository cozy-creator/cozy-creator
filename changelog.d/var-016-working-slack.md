A model whose weights don't fit beside its working memory keeps more of them on the card. A loop now gives working memory beyond its measured need back to its weights rather than re-reading them every step: H3 Turbo 15 s on a 24 GB card re-reads 1-10 GB a step instead of 20. And a call that runs short of memory while idle executors of other packages sit on the GPU has TensorD end one before it evicts its own weights: Ideogram 4 next to idle MOSS and Anima executors had been paging both of its models every step.
