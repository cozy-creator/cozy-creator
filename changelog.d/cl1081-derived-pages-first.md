TensorFS: a short disk drops derived pages (LoRA bakes) before any model, least recently used first, whatever lane keeps them, and keeping one never evicts a model or ends an executor; one a reader has open stays. Before, a model in use kept every bake it ever made: kamitsure's in-use H3 held 61 GB of bakes on a full 200 GB disk (cl-1081). A varena page-in that reads a block again reads only this disk, never the network.
