TensorD: a disk below its reserve ends as many idle executors as the reserve and the bytes coming in need, so their models are evicted; before, it ended them only for the bytes coming in, often one whose model alone couldn't make the room, and evicted nothing. A run's room counts the reserve too: refused before it starts when even every unused model can't make it, never mid-download. The 10-minute sweep ends idle executors on a low disk before it lets go of held outputs: kamitsure (11-16) kept an idle LTX's 71.5 GB at its reserve and let outputs go (cl-1076).
