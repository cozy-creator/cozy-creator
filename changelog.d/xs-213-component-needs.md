A package may rule a model argument's component (`needs={"model.vision": …}`, the slot's `component_needs`): a call whose rule doesn't hold fetches none of that component. TensorD's walk (`needs::wants`) names them per slot (`Wants::unread`) and its fetch leaves them out; Calcifer's quote leaves their chunks out too, by the checkpoint's header. A component with no rule, or whose rule gives no answer, comes with its slot (xs-213).
