An executor past varena's host number asks idle executors for their pages again, so an uncapped model keeps its weights and baked LoRA pages in host RAM across requests (tfs-299). Since idle executors stopped looking on a timer, one whose host tier was past the number asked no one and let every page of its own go: on a 62 GB RTX 4090 host Ideogram 4 kept nothing beside an idle Flux-2 and read 40 GB from disk each request, and Flux-2 Turbo let 27 GB of baked pages go and baked them again. Past the number the tier now asks idle executors for the shortfall and lets go only its own excess.
