TensorFS spends less of a load outside its copies (cl-1318): on overlayfs (a container's root, where pods keep the store) a read learns how much of each range the page cache holds from at most 8 of its pages, where `mincore(2)` over every page of every chunk cost 17-24 ms a GB on an RTX 4090 pod; a staged piece reads its file in one request, opens each file's O_DIRECT description once a read and reuses one bounce buffer a reader; the H2D ceiling key and boot id are made once a process. `release-gate/sessions/tfs-bound.sh` measures a load against its machine's bound (disk and pinned H2D), cold from disk and from RAM, a whole-resident model and H3's paged transformer, per TensorFS.
