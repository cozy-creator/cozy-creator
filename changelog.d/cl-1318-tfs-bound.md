A model loads closer to its machine's bound (cl-1318). A read keeps the weights it reads in RAM: it goes past the page cache only when memory nothing pins or holds couldn't keep them, where a cache full of older bytes sent every later read past it (H3's text encoder in a fresh executor read 9.6 GB of 26.4 again from disk), and then a range the cache partly holds reads only its holes from disk. On overlayfs (a container's root, where pods keep the store) a read learns how much of a range the cache holds from at most 8 of its pages, where `mincore(2)` over every page of every chunk cost 17-24 ms a GB: an RTX 4090 pod's whole-model fill from RAM went from 13.7 to 21.5 GB/s. A staged piece reads its file in one request. The disk ceiling every bound divides by measures the disk where the TensorFS home holds few bytes (it read 6e-06 GB/s there), and a load's own rate only raises it. `release-gate/sessions/tfs-bound.sh` measures a load against its machine's bound, cold from disk and from RAM, a whole-resident model and H3's paged transformer, per TensorFS.
