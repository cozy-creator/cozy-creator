A run's time left is never less than the bytes it moves now (a download, a model loading) left at their rate. With nothing else known, that is its time left, said as "at least" (`eta_basis: bytes`), never none while bytes move at a known rate. A live run's machine GPU is on every record and lifecycle event (`run.in_progress`) as it goes, replayed ones too, not only on its record and at its end.
