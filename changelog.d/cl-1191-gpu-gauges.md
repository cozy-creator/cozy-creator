Machines shows how hard each GPU is working, live, on every machine's row: its busy share as a bar with its last minute beside it, its memory, power against its limit, temperature, and "Power-limited" or "Thermal" when its driver holds it back. A machine's drawer shows the same a row each, with its clock. `cozy machine show` prints each GPU's numbers now, live in a terminal. Machines read their GPUs only while a page shows them (cl-1191).
