`cozy open` opens the project in a private window of your own browser (`$BROWSER`'s, else your default's, else an installed Chrome, Brave, Edge or Firefox), so its pages stay out of your browser's history and its files out of its cache, and prints its address; with no browser that has a private window it opens nothing and says so in one line. Cozy no longer starts its own Chrome, so a Calcifer restart or update leaves your tabs open: they reconnect, and an encrypted project's shows its locked page. Before a lock, a storage change or a stop closes an encrypted project, its open tabs send what was just typed first (cl-1067).
