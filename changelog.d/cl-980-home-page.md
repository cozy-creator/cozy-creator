The web page's home: `/` shows every project along the top, most recently opened first, over an empty canvas that says to pick one or press +. The + makes a project in place: its folder suggested as `<the last project's parent>/untitled-N` and edited where it is, encrypted unless you choose otherwise, its recovery secret shown once, then opened. A folder that isn't empty, or can't be made, is said in one line beside the field. The Projects drawer and a first drop on an empty canvas make one the same way, and there's one create: `POST /v1/projects {path}`, as `cozy new`; a create by name alone is gone. Going from one project to another keeps the page's frame and never blanks the screen.
