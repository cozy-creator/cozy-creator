TensorFS reads a range through the page cache when the cache holds at least half of it, or has room for what it lacks (half the host's limit kept free, as TensorD's read-ahead keeps it), decided once per read rather than per 1 MiB piece; only a store the cache can't hold goes O_DIRECT. A mostly cached store no longer goes to the disk whole (hidream-refs on an RTX 4090 from a store 87% cached: step 1 2.91 s against 4.85 s before and v1's 3.67, runs 40275, 40252, 40288), and a cold read stays cached for the next one.
