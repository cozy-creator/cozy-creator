A mention of an LLM box reads its answer (xs-204). `<name>` in a prompt sends that box's state, whatever it is: a picture or video as its file, a Text box's words, and now an LLM box's answer as words in place: its approved one when pinned, else its current one, and `<name.N>` that version. A new answer puts what reads it out of date, and a render of what reads an LLM box with no answer yet makes the answer first.
