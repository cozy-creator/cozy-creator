An LLM tool's words appear on its box as they are written, at rest and in Focus, for a typed prompt and for a caption of a picture or video; its version lands as before. Calcifer also answers OpenAI's Responses API for other programs (base URL `http://<Calcifer>/v1/openai`: `responses`, streamed or not, and `models`), with the CLI's bearer as the API key. `cozy run` writes an LLM's words on stdout as they come (Thinking… while it thinks).
