A new rental no longer stays on a host whose link is far too slow. Its machine holds its first download to the rule its Hub names (200 Mbit/s): under it for 180 s with no minute back at it, within 15 minutes of starting and with under 8 GiB on its disk, it tells the Hub once, and the Hub ends that host and places the rental on another, at most twice. Its runs return to Cozy's queue as their next attempt, saying "its host downloaded too slowly", and `cozy machine ls` shows it "moving to a faster host" until it serves again. A machine that already holds models, or one past its first 15 minutes, is never ended for its link; like any host that keeps pace, it reports the pace its link sustained, once, so the Hub buys faster hosts next time. The rate counts only while the link sets the pace: a lone small file, a stalled read or a download sharing the link with an install is not judged.
