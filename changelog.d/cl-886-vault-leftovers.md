Encrypting a project leaves nothing behind: the unencrypted original, the conversion's stage, its receipt and its mount folders under `~/.cozy/calcifer/vaults/` are all removed, and a conversion interrupted midway is cleaned up at the next start or open. `cozy vault encrypt --preserve-plaintext` and the page's "Keep an unencrypted copy" option are gone. Calcifer never opens its own `.cozy-*` folders as projects, and a copied project folder that takes a new identity says so.
