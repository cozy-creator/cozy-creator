A project's chosen machines that ended drop out of its list, and the page stops counting them at once. When every chosen machine is gone (ended, ending or stopped by its provider), a render or `cozy run` goes where it finishes soonest and says why, instead of being refused; with only this computer left, the page asks first and offers to rent. A run sent the same second its machine releases itself is placed again instead of failing. Rentals with a capped GPU memory (test pods) are never used automatically.
