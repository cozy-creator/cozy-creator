A run whose executor dies of SIGSEGV, SIGBUS, SIGABRT, SIGFPE or SIGILL fails `executor_crashed`, "executor crashed: SIGSEGV in <frame>" (the frame from the Runtime's crash record), with the crashed thread's native frames in its detail (`cozy run watch`), not `executor_invalidated` "the executor ended on its own". Machines keep executor cores: a `core_pattern` directory the container lacks (vast) is made, each core is capped at half the free disk (at most 32 GiB), only the newest stays, and the machine's log names it (cr-275).
