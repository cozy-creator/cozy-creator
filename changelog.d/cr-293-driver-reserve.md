TensorFS leaves the driver 512 MiB of the card free at every map, for what it allocates on its own (a kernel's code at its first launch, local memory, a library's handle). A full card had failed a cold H3's first `index_select` on a 5090. When the pages fit but not beside the reserve, the map evicts only what is idle and done, and short of that it takes the reserve: the reserve never makes a demand wait or fail.
