TensorFS leaves the driver 512 MiB of the card free at every map, for what it allocates on its own (a kernel's code at its first launch, local memory, a library's handle). A full card had failed a cold H3's first `index_select` on a 5090. After such a failure `tensorfs.varena.torch.driver_short` doubles the reserve and gives up what the process can; a demand with every other lever gone still takes the reserve before it fails.
