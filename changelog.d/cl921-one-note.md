A run says one thing at most, and only what matters. A render that completes with warnings (a Runtime's handled fallback, an adjustment, its result's) puts none of them in a note: they're on its box, under its Warnings. A render that fails says so in one note, with its error to copy, that stays until it's dismissed; so does a run that couldn't be sent. Sending a run says what it has to say in one note.
