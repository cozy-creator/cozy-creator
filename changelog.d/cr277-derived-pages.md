TensorFS keeps derived pages on disk and reads pages ahead (cr-277). A page a Source's derive made (a LoRA baked into a block) and the host tier keeps is written to the store behind the fill, as cache in its checkpoint's lane under `derived/<slot>/<page>`; a later process reads it there into a pinned page instead of reading the stored bytes and the LoRA's factors and baking again, and can read it ahead of demand, which a derive never could. `Pages.stage(sources)` reads pages into the tier ahead of their use on its own thread, within free room: a slot's derived page, else a derive's stored bytes (its fill takes them instead of the store), else a plain page's. New record origins `derived` and `staged`; the tier's stats count `staged`, `persisted`, `persist_skipped`, `persist_failed`.
