A lost machine never fails a job. Calcifer keeps the queue of jobs, and a machine is only where a job is assigned now. When a machine ends, vanishes or stops answering before it takes a job, the job goes back to the queue and is placed again wherever it finishes soonest: the order's replacement host, another rental, or this computer. A job that was running there runs again elsewhere as its next attempt ("attempt 1 stopped: stand-r shut down; attempt 2 of 3 goes wherever it finishes soonest"). A job fails only for its own error, a cancel, or when a third machine is lost under it.
