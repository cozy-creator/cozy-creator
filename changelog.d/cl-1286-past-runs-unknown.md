A function's past runs, on a machine, on this computer's history and on other machines, are scaled to the size asked as its rows are: by its formula's times at both sizes where it declares one, else by each size variable's exponent fitted over its rows (a 210-word Qwen Image 2 prompt after a 9-word run is about 2.2 times as long, not 23). Estimates may count a text's words (`words(text)`). A time nothing measured is unknown, never a minute assumed (`UNKNOWN_MS` removed): a machine with an estimate wins over one without; with none anywhere, the job goes where it starts soonest and its time shows as unknown (`cozy run watch`'s placed line, the page's estimate). `cozy package publish` cuts a benchmark note over the Hub's 200 bytes to fit at a character, saying so once, instead of the Hub refusing the release.
