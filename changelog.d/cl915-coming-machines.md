Placement counts machines that are coming up. A rental still booting or just ordered gets a finish: when it should serve (its Hub's boot history for its provider and card, and the phase it's in), then its install and weights, the runs already sent to it, and the job on its card. A job waits for one that finishes it sooner than any machine up, and its box says so ("waits for pluto, ready in about 2 min"); one that will be later never holds it. `cozy run --on new` (and the page's "a new machine") rents nothing when a machine up or on its way would finish sooner: it goes there and says why. Runs on rentals tell their Hub what they took, so a card's times are known before a machine of it runs.
