Boxes copy, paste and duplicate as in Figma: Ctrl/Cmd+C and Ctrl/Cmd+V (at the pointer, else beside what was copied), Ctrl/Cmd+D, Alt/Option and a drag, and Duplicate on a box's menu. A copy is a new box (`hei_2`) with the same tool, words, versions and approval, sharing their files with no bytes copied; a mention between boxes copied together names the copy. One Undo takes them back.
