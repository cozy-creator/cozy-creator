A deleted box is gone from the project. Nothing offers or resolves its name: `<` doesn't suggest it, a mention of it is struck "was deleted", and a render or `cozy run` that names it is refused, free. Its name is free for a new box. Undo brings it back for 3 hours; then its files are deleted from the project (`files/`, thumbnails, clips), unless another box holds the same file, whether or not the page is open, and Undo says it can no longer be restored. Boxes deleted before this release go the first time their project opens.
