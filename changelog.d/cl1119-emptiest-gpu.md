A model that needs a new executor goes to the GPU with the most room (cl-1119). TensorD took the lowest-numbered GPU whose call slot was free, so on a 2× RTX A6000 Qwen3.6-35B-A3B went beside Qwen3.5-9B on GPU 0, to 48.2 of 48 GB, while GPU 1 sat empty; a second Qwen3.6 call then built a second executor on GPU 1 while the first waited behind Qwen3.5's call. Now a GPU's room is its size less what its executors hold, and an empty GPU comes before one keeping another model's weights warm.
