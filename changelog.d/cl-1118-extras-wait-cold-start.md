A machine's background downloads (a call's extras, prefetched weights, warm sets) now wait while a call there starts cold, from its executor's start until its models are loaded, then go on at once. Before, they waited only while a run's needs downloaded: a cold MiniMax H3 call on an RTX 5090 whose link ran at 270 MB/s took 167 s from its executor's start to its GPU up, against 1 s, while 22 GB of extras downloaded beside it.
