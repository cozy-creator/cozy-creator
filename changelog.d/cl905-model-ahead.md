A running job's on-demand model no longer waits on its executor's slow read or on a later job's download. An executor reads a model it builds on demand (Qwen Image's prompt expander) through its own fetch, a few requests at a time, outside the machine's download line: on one rental the expander's 8-10 GB came at about 40 MB/s while H3's fetch ahead took the rest of the link. Now when another process sharing the machine's store begins fetching a model a started call builds on demand, TensorD fetches the rest of that model itself, ahead of every queued job's download with all its requests; the executor takes those bytes as it reads, and other downloads go on with what the link has left.
