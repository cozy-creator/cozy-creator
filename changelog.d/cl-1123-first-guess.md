Calcifer: a box's first guess is faster, and an edit doesn't wait for one (cl-1123). A quote now reads its models' cards in parallel, then their headers in parallel; before, it read them one at a time. A guess reads its package once. A guess slower than 250 ms logs its stages in `cozy machine logs local calcifer`. An edit made while a guess round is still reading starts its own round; the newest round's guesses stand. On a CPU runner, Qwen Image 2.1's first guess on a picture took 0.7 s instead of 2.5 s, and an edit typed during it reached its machine 0.7 s after it was typed, instead of 2.6 s.
