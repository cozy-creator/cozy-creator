TensorFS device reads run side by side (cr-318): a read takes its staging whole and keeps a failed reader's slots with its own owners, copies on lanes of its own, runs its readers on kept threads, and recovery and drain are exclusive, where every read on a device waited for the one before. Two threads of small reads: 4.0–4.7 → 4.9–6.0 GB/s on an RTX 3070; a paged decode's reads 3.3 → 4.4 GB/s on an RTX A6000.
