TensorFS knows LTX-2.5 (`ltx-2-5`, read from Cozy Creator's mirror of Lightricks' gated release) and reads its LoRAs and IC-LoRAs (`ltx2.lora`): LTX's native names under `diffusion_model.` (Lightricks', ComfyUI's, musubi-tuner's, AI-Toolkit's), Diffusers', PEFT's and kohya's, stored as a LoRA of its `transformer` in Diffusers' module names. LTX-2.3's LoRAs are read too: their linears are 2.5's. An IC-LoRA's `reference_downscale_factor` is in its adapter config.
