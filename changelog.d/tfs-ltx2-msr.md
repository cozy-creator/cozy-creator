TensorFS reads LiconStudio's Multiple-Subject Reference LoRAs for LTX-2.5 (`ltx2.lora`): a converter table names whole modules an adapter may carry beside its factors, which are stored as PEFT's `modules_to_save` (their own keys, the config listing them), here MSR's two reference-slot embeddings; an adapter's `reference_*` metadata is in its adapter config, typed (booleans, numbers, strings).
